# Keep Your Credentials

A lightweight, cross-platform credentials manager for Linux and Windows.

- **Encrypted on your device.** Your vault is encrypted with a key derived from your master password (Argon2id, XChaCha20-Poly1305) before it is written to disk or uploaded anywhere.
- **Your own Google Drive, optional.** To use the same vault on several devices, connect your own Google account. The vault is stored, still encrypted, in a hidden folder of your Drive that only this app can see.
- **Organized.** Folders, tags and an instant search across names, URLs, tags, folders and descriptions.
- **Safe clipboard.** Copied passwords are cleared after 30 seconds and kept out of clipboard history.
- **Signed updates.** The app checks for new versions, downloads them in the background, verifies their signature and installs only when you choose.

## Download

Get the latest version from the [releases page](https://github.com/JeanKouss/keep-your-credentials-public-releases/releases/latest):

- **Windows:** `Keep.Your.Credentials_<version>_x64-setup.exe`
- **Linux:** `Keep.Your.Credentials_<version>_amd64.AppImage` (make it executable; on Arch, install `fuse2`)

## Privacy

Keep Your Credentials has no servers, no accounts of its own and no analytics. Read the [privacy policy](privacy).
